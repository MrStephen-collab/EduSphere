## PROJECT: SCHOOL DIGITAL LEARNING, ASSESSMENT & ENGAGEMENT PLATFORM

### PROJECT CODENAME

`EduSphere` — use this as the temporary internal project name. Make the product name configurable so it can be changed later without restructuring the application.

---

# 1. YOUR ROLE

You are the lead software architect, senior full-stack engineer, UI/UX designer, database engineer, security engineer, DevOps engineer and QA engineer responsible for building this production-ready SaaS application.

Do not build a simple prototype.

Build a scalable, modern, secure, mobile-first, multi-tenant SaaS platform that can eventually serve thousands of Nigerian schools and hundreds of thousands of students.

The application must be:

* Production-ready
* Responsive
* Mobile-first
* PWA-ready
* Multi-tenant
* Secure
* Fast
* Accessible
* SEO-friendly where applicable
* Easy to maintain
* Modular
* Scalable
* Designed for Nigerian schools
* Ready for future Android/iOS applications
* Ready for future AI functionality

Do not implement fake functionality where real functionality is reasonably possible.

Do not leave major features as placeholders.

Where a third-party service is required, create a clean provider abstraction and environment-variable configuration.

---

# 2. PRODUCT VISION

The platform gives every school its own digital academic environment.

The core concept is:

> YOUR SCHOOL. YOUR STUDENTS. YOUR DIGITAL CLASSROOM.

Each school gets its own branded environment where:

Teachers can:

* Create lessons
* Upload teaching materials
* Add videos
* Create assignments
* Create quizzes
* Build CBT examinations
* Manage question banks
* Grade submissions
* Monitor student performance

Students can:

* Access lessons
* Watch educational videos
* Read learning materials
* Practise questions
* Submit assignments
* Take CBT examinations
* View results
* Track academic progress

Parents can:

* Monitor their children's learning
* View results
* Monitor assignments
* Receive academic notifications
* See performance trends

School administrators can:

* Manage students
* Manage teachers
* Manage classes
* Manage subjects
* Manage academic sessions
* Monitor learning activity
* Monitor examinations
* View academic analytics

The platform should eventually become the school's:

> Digital Classroom + LMS + CBT + Assessment + Academic Engagement Platform.

# 3. IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING

Do NOT position this product as merely:

"An e-learning platform."

Do NOT build it like a generic file storage system.

The platform must follow this academic workflow:

Teacher creates content

↓

Student learns

↓

Student practises

↓

Student completes assignment

↓

Student takes assessment

↓

System marks assessment

↓

Student receives result

↓

Teacher sees performance

↓

Parent sees progress

↓

School identifies weak areas

This learning loop is the heart of the product.

# 54. ROW LEVEL SECURITY

Every school-owned table must have RLS enabled.

Users can only access records belonging to their school.

Examples:

A teacher from School A cannot query:

```text
School B students
School B courses
School B results
```

A parent can only access their linked children.

A student can only access:

* Their own results
* Their assigned classes
* Their assigned courses
* Their own submissions
* Their own attempts
